# Rights, Provenance, and Adaptation

## Rights inventory

Track every asset:

| Asset | Creator | Tool/source | Human contribution | Rights status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Character design | Steven | Original/AI-assisted | Selection, revision, costume, pose | Verify |
| Story | Steven | Original writing | Plot, dialogue, structure | Owned if original |
| Music | Steven/collaborator | Tool or recording | Lyrics, arrangement, direction | Check license |
| Font | Third party | Font provider | Layout use | Check license |
| Background | Original/stock/AI | Source record | Editing and composition | Verify |
| Logo | Steven | Design workflow | Mark and typography | Search/register |

## AI-assisted work

Preserve:

- Prompts
- Generations
- Rejected alternatives
- Sketches
- Edits
- Composites
- Human-written text
- Layout decisions
- Final art direction

Do not claim control over third-party characters, logos, uniforms, symbols, music, or recognizable franchise elements.

## Deal ladder

### Shopping agreement

Limited permission to take the project to buyers for a defined term.

### Option

A producer receives exclusive development rights for a defined period. Require option fee, extension fee, scope, term, reversion, credit, consultation, and purchase price.

### Adaptation license

You retain underlying IP while granting specific adaptation rights.

### Assignment

You transfer ownership. Use only with professional legal advice.
