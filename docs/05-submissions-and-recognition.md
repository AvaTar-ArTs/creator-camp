# Submission, Competition, and Recognition Routes

## Best-fit routes

### MANGA Plus Creators

Global submission platform connected to Shueisha. English and Spanish submissions are supported, with monthly awards and possible publication on MANGA Plus.

Prepare:

- Finished manga or substantial excerpt
- Cover
- Creator profile
- Original rights
- Clean reading order

### VIZ Originals

Accepts one-shots and ongoing manga series in English. Strong fit for a polished English-language pilot with a clear commercial hook.

### Kodansha Manga Academy

International scouting and editor-discovery route. Monitor global award announcements and direct-submission requirements. Traditional Japanese manga awards often require Japanese-language materials.

### Tezuka Award / Akatsuka Award

Tezuka: story manga. Akatsuka: gag/comedy manga. Verify language, format, page, and submission rules on the current official portal before preparing the entry.

### Japan International MANGA Award

International recognition for eligible non-Japanese creators. Published and unpublished work may be accepted, but page count, creation date, originality, and submission windows change annually.

### Open publication platforms

- WEBTOON CANVAS
- Tapas
- GlobalComix
- Royal Road
- Scribble Hub

Use these for audience evidence, not as substitutes for a finished submission package.

## Recognition strategy

Submit different assets to different opportunities:

| Asset | Best target |
|---|---|
| One-shot manga | MANGA Plus, VIZ, Tezuka, international manga awards |
| Serialized prose | Royal Road, Tapas, Scribble Hub |
| Character art | Illustration contests, conventions, portfolio platforms |
| Worldbuilding | Artbooks, creator showcases, pitch festivals |
| Trailer/animatic | Producer meetings, crowdfunding, creator campaigns |
| Finished volume | KDP, Kobo, direct store, conventions |

## Timing rule

Do not publish every page publicly before a contest that requires unpublished work. Check first-publication and exclusivity terms before releasing a pilot.
