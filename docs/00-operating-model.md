# Creator Camp Operating Model

## Objective

Develop an original entertainment property that can survive as prose, visual storytelling, serialized content, and screen adaptation.

## The six layers

1. **Canon** — master story, characters, world rules, timeline, and rights records.
2. **Serialization** — recurring web releases that create readership and feedback.
3. **Packaging** — revised light-novel volumes, manga pilots, ebooks, print books, and artbooks.
4. **Audience** — owned website, newsletter, community, analytics, and direct sales.
5. **Recognition** — contests, publisher submissions, conventions, reviews, and creator profiles.
6. **Adaptation** — pitch bible, season outline, animatic, producer outreach, and rights negotiation.

## Flagship rule

Do not launch an entire archive as one project. Select one property with:

- A one-sentence hook
- A protagonist with an urgent contradiction
- A repeatable story engine
- A visually distinctive cast
- A conflict that can escalate across volumes
- A format that can be completed on schedule

## Evidence ladder

| Stage | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Concept | Logline, premise, moodboard |
| Prototype | 1–3 chapters, character sheets |
| Proof | 10-chapter season or 30–45 page pilot |
| Audience | Completion, return readers, followers, comments |
| Commercial | Preorders, sales, crowdfunding, memberships |
| Industry | Awards, editor feedback, publisher/producer interest |
| Adaptation | Rights-clean bible, budgetable season, pitch meetings |

## Recommended first campaign

- 20,000–30,000 words of prepared web fiction
- 10 public chapters
- 6–10 original illustrations
- 1 cover
- 1 character/world bible
- 1 manga pilot adaptation
- 1 revised ebook edition
- 1 owned landing page and mailing list

## Canon policy

Maintain three versions:

- **Master canon:** private, editable source of truth
- **Public serial:** released chapters and public-facing extras
- **Commercial edition:** revised, edited, illustrated, and packaged volume

Never allow platform formatting or reader comments to become the only copy of the work.
