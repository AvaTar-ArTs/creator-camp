# Multiformat Adaptation Matrix

The goal is not mechanical conversion. Each medium gets an authored treatment while preserving canon, psychology, event order, and visual identity.

| Format | Strength | Canon source | Adaptation work |
|---|---|---|---|
| Web fiction | speed and feedback | story and scene graph | chapter rhythm and hooks |
| Light novel | revised prose and illustrations | approved manuscript | editing and packaging |
| Manga | visual acting and page composition | scene contracts | panels, dialogue, lettering |
| Webtoon | mobile scroll and reveal timing | scene/panel plan | vertical pacing |
| Audiobook | voice and atmosphere | manuscript and character bible | narration, casting, sound |
| Motion comic | art plus camera and sound | cells and shot plan | parallax and timing |
| Anime pitch | scalable screen story | bible and season plan | episode and budget structure |
| Game | agency and replay | world rules and faction graph | mechanics and progression |

## Per-format packet

- Canon reference
- Format outline
- Character state at entry
- Scene list
- Required visual anchors
- Dialogue and lettering rules
- Rights record
- Source/version IDs
- Review checklist
- Export metadata
