# Comparative Study Framework

Use favorite anime as structural study material, never as a source of copied characters, names, symbols, or protected expression.

## JoJo's Bizarre Adventure

Study:

- Generational IP design
- Distinct saga identities
- Fashion and silhouette
- Music and cultural references
- Puzzle-like battles
- Villain philosophy
- Geographic and genre shifts

Application: build separate arcs with different protagonists, locations, aesthetics, and conflict engines inside one mythology.

## Solo Leveling

Study:

- Simple high-concept premise
- Visible progression
- Rank and ability systems
- Cliffhanger serialization
- Strong visual transformations
- Web-novel-to-webtoon-to-anime movement

Application: create a measurable curse, rank, class, or transformation system with costs.

## That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime

Study:

- Web-fiction origin
- Nation building
- Faction growth
- Monster catalog
- Side-story potential
- Mascot and merchandise design
- Multi-format expansion

Application: design a world with politics, resources, factions, creatures, humor, tragedy, and spin-offs.

## Blue Exorcist

Study:

- Ensemble readability
- School and institutional setting
- Religious systems
- Family trauma
- Demon-human contradiction
- Specialist roles
- Human life embedded inside fantasy

Application: make supernatural worldbuilding operate through believable institutions and personal relationships.

## Creator Camp synthesis

```
JoJo: saga identity
+ Solo Leveling: progression engine
+ Slime: expansion ecosystem
+ Blue Exorcist: ensemble and institutions
= original AvatarArts flagship IP
```

The final property must have its own premise, iconography, character psychology, visual language, and world rules.
